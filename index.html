RiskGuard — fraud scoring at forty milliseconds.

Banks need to catch fraud in real time without blocking legitimate customers. Manual review is too slow. Pure-ML systems are hard to explain to compliance — and to a customer whose transaction just got declined at a checkout.

What I did

Wrote an end-to-end PRD for a rules-plus-scoring engine with a hard latency budget of <40ms P99. Specified the API contract, weighted-signal logic, decision thresholds (approve / review / decline), and the rules-trace payload analysts see when they review a flagged transaction.

Product decisions worth calling out

The middle review band matters more than most designs treat it — that’s where you protect against false positives that erode customer trust. And explainability isn’t a nice-to-have in fraud: the rules trace is a first-class product feature, not a debug log.

Deliverable: Complete PRD covering scoring logic, API contract, analyst dashboard, alerts flow, MVP scope, and release criteria — modeled against a target 42% reduction in fraud losses versus a rules-only baseline.
Year 2025
Role End-to-end: PRD to shipped MVP
Domain AI · Enterprise · RAG
View the project →

An AI assistant that will say “I don’t know.”

Construction site teams spend hours searching PDFs — contracts, safety manuals, method statements — for answers they need in minutes. On a construction site, a wrong answer is worse than no answer.

What I did

Scoped a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system with a hard product constraint: never answer without a source, and explicitly say “not found” when the retrieved context can’t support a confident answer. Defined ingestion, chunking, retrieval, and generation as four separate quality gates with their own evaluation criteria. Shipped the MVP end-to-end with FastAPI, LangChain, ChromaDB, and Docker.

Product decision worth calling out

Trust beats coverage. It’s better for the assistant to refuse 20% of questions than to hallucinate a safety clause and get someone hurt. That single principle shaped every downstream decision — chunk size, retrieval top-k, the prompt, the “not found” UX.

Outcome: Working MVP that answers questions with document + page references, refuses when unsure, and runs locally or in Docker with no manual infrastructure setup.
Year 2024
Role Founder & Product Lead
Domain EdTech · 0-to-1 · Tier 2/3

Aspire — what Tier 2/3 JEE aspirants actually want.

Kids in Tier 2/3 cities preparing for JEE are stuck between offline coaching that costs a lakh they don’t have and online platforms built for kids who already know what they’re doing.

What I did

Talked to twenty-plus students and parents. Modeled unit economics against realistic willingness-to-pay in Tier 2/3 markets. Designed the onboarding funnel for students who might be the first in their family to attempt JEE. Ran a localized GTM pilot to test whether paid social or word-of-mouth referral converted better in a specific district.

What I learned

Willingness-to-pay wasn’t the blocker. The trust gap was. Parents weren’t skeptical of the price — they were skeptical of anything online after a decade of bad experiences with sketchy edtech. That reframed the whole product: the wedge had to be trust, not features or price.

Outcome: Validated the core wedge, killed the general-purpose version, and set the foundation for a trust-first onboarding experience.
